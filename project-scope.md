    # Grocery List App

## 1. Problem Statement

A friend of yours goes grocery shopping every week and keeps forgetting what she needs — she wants a dead-simple app on her phone to jot items down before she leaves, and tick them off while she's walking the aisles with patchy signal. She's not technical. She just wants it to work without thinking.

## 2. Solution

Create an android App which can be worked on any android mobile device which is old upto 15 years, without internet connection. we have make a very simple application, we can add item in list then mark it as brought or not brought, and can remove it and when we close the app and open again it should be same as it was before.

## 3. Target Audience

-   Non-technical users who want a simple grocery list app.
-   Users who want to use the app without an internet connection.
-   Users who want to use the app on old android devices.

## 4. Condition

- Kotlin, native Android (no Flutter/React Native)
- Must work fully offline — no network calls of any kind
- Minimum SDK: your choice, but state it and say why
- No third-party UI libraries — use standard Android views/Compose only
- App must not crash on any of the three actions (add/tick/remove),including with an empty or very long item name
